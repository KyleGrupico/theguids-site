Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TheGuids handles information when its account owner uses its publishing tools and official platform integrations.

Information We Process

When a TikTok account is connected through TikTok's official authorization process, we may process limited account information, including:

  • TikTok account identifier
  • Display name and username
  • Granted authorization permissions
  • Account and publishing capabilities
  • Authorization expiration information

We may also process information necessary to prepare and upload authorized content, including captions, hashtags, privacy selections, disclosure settings, approval records, media metadata, upload status, and platform response information.

How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate the connected account
  • Display the account receiving the content
  • Prepare and upload authorized content
  • Check processing and publishing status
  • Prevent accidental duplicate uploads
  • Maintain security and troubleshoot errors

Authentication Credentials

Access and refresh credentials are stored securely and are not displayed publicly. Credentials are used only to perform actions authorized by the connected account owner through official platform APIs.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to authenticate accounts and provide requested publishing functions through TikTok's official services.

TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the publishing tools, maintain security, prevent duplicates, and satisfy applicable obligations.

A connected account may be disconnected or its authorization revoked. Requests concerning stored account information or deletion may be submitted through our Contact page.

Children's Privacy

TheGuids publishing tools are not intended for use by children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as our services, integrations, or legal obligations change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be submitted through our Contact page.
